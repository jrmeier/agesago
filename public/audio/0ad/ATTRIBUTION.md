The files were downloaded from the official archived **0ad/0ad** Git repository,
revision `61a3b9507d974084e6badb88a0826bd89a6d5b8b`, directory
`binaries/data/mods/public/audio/music/`. The accompanying upstream audio license
is preserved verbatim in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt). Pinned source URLs, original
and bundled SHA-256 checksums, durations and sizes are in [provenance.json](provenance.json).

Changes: full recordings re-encoded from upstream Ogg Vorbis to MP3 stereo
at 96 kbit/s, with title/artist/license metadata.
There are no cuts or composition edits. Ages Ago overlaps track and loop joins
for two seconds and crossfades between calm and battle playback. These recordings
and re-encodings remain CC BY-SA 3.0. No endorsement by the creators is implied.

Ages Ago's sound effects remain generated in the browser and are separate from
these music recordings.
